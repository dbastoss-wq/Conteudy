## Tool comparison

Tools for planning, running and measuring Google Ads campaigns and creating the content behind them.

**Pricing key:** **Free** means no paid plan is needed. **Free + Premium** means a usable free tier, with paid plans unlocking more. **Premium** means paid plans only (some offer a trial). Pricing models change often, so check each site for current plans.

### Google Ads & PPC

| Tool Name | Link | Description | Pricing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Ads | [ads.google.com](https://ads.google.com) | Google's ad platform for Search, Display, YouTube, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns, with automated bidding and conversion tracking | Free (you pay for ad spend) |
| Google Keyword Planner | [ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/) | Keyword ideas, monthly search volumes and top-of-page bid estimates pulled straight from Google Ads data | Free (requires a Google Ads account) |
| Google Ads Editor | [ads.google.com/home/tools/ads-editor](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/ads-editor/) | Desktop app for bulk-editing campaigns, ad groups and ads offline, then posting all changes at once | Free |
| Google Ads Transparency Center | [adstransparency.google.com](https://adstransparency.google.com) | Look up any advertiser and see the ads they run across Search, YouTube and Display, filtered by region and date | Free |
| Optmyzr | [optmyzr.com](https://www.optmyzr.com) | PPC management suite with one-click optimizations, rule-based automation, budget pacing and client reporting for Google and Microsoft Ads | Premium |
